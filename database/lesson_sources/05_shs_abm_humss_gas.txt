- Users include owners, investors, lenders, and the government.

- Accounting helps businesses make informed decisions.
? List three users of accounting information and what each needs to know.
2 | The Accounting Equation | Every transaction keeps the accounting equation in balance.
- Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity.
- Assets are resources owned; liabilities are debts owed.
- Owner's equity is the owner's claim on the assets.
? If assets are ₱500,000 and liabilities are ₱200,000, find owner's equity.

4 | The Accounting Cycle | The accounting cycle is the step-by-step process of handling business transactions.
- Analyze transactions and record them in the journal.
- Post entries to the ledger and prepare a trial balance.
- Prepare financial statements and close the books.
? Arrange the steps of the accounting cycle in order.
5 | Journalizing Transactions | Journal entries record transactions using debits and credits.
- Every entry has at least one debit and one credit.
- Total debits must equal total credits.
- Assets increase with a debit; liabilities and equity increase with a credit.
